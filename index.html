Team

3 groups

Twelve researchers in three groups — one in a NATO country and two in a NATO partner country — covering catalysis, materials synthesis and advanced characterisation.

Project Overview

Catalysing Clean Energy & Technological Resilience

Chemical sciences · Clean energy

Addressing energy and environmental security through international scientific cooperation on catalysis, metal hydrides and green fuel production.

Research Topics

04

The four tasks of the work plan: establishing the catalytic testing conditions, screening three families of metal hydrides, and scaling up the selected catalyst.
